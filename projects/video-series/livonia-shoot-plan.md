## 3. Pre-interviews (15 min each, phone)

Purpose: build each outline from real material, and hear who's a natural on camera.
If all Joe supplies is names, Michael's team can run these solo; Joe will join where
bandwidth allows — flagging now: **Joe should personally join the ASC pre-interview**
given the staffing sensitivities.

Ask every subject:
1. Walk me through what a patient experiences with you, first contact to done.
2. What do patients most misunderstand or worry about before they see you?
3. What's different about doing this *here* vs. anywhere else? (listen for "one roof,"
   coordination, same-week — our verified differentiators, in their words)
4. Tell me about a moment a patient surprised you. (story-mining; testimonial leads)
5. What question do you answer ten times a week? (feeds the question bank — these
   become the on-camera questions)
6. Anything we should NOT say or show? (clinical/compliance flags per department)

Department-specific adds:
- **PT:** Which surgeries do you see most (knee replacement? spine?)? How does working
  in the same building as the surgeons actually change a patient's rehab?
- **Pharmacy:** What does "meds before you leave" actually look like? How do you
  coordinate with the surgeon/PT teams? (No controlled-substance specifics.)
- **ASC:** Day-of-surgery walk-through, arrival to discharge. Who's a candidate for
  outpatient vs. hospital? What does "minimally invasive" mean here in practice?

## 4. Video outlines — the bullet points to hit

Shared spec: 2:00 target · shoot 16:9 **and** capture 9:16 for social · natural
straight-on framing · single Synergy end card (never the legacy Mendelson cards) ·
lower-third with name + credentials · say brand + "in Livonia" once each.

**Language rules from the 8/6 call:** never say "integrated care model" on camera —
it's corporate speak. **Show** the handoff instead, and say it warm: "under one roof,"
first names, "they walked me across the hall." What production keeps hearing from
patients: *intimacy, above-and-beyond, everyone's on a first-name basis ("it's Sue")* —
that's the register. And never disparage hospital systems on camera; make the contrast
by describing us (personal, first-name, carried through the whole process), not them.
