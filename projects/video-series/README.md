## Why this series matters (the data case)

- **We have zero video search presence.** GSC video-search results for the last 90 days:
  no rows. No VideoObject schema on any of 423 pages, no transcripts (Cardinal audit).
- **1.18M organic impressions/quarter, 1.15% CTR** — 58% of impressions are non-branded
  researchers who never click. Videos that answer their questions are the missing format.
- The five foundational spine conditions (stenosis, herniated disc, sciatica, DDD,
  spondylolisthesis) have **zero** content coverage — round-2 testimonials + the ASC
  video are the fastest way to open those fronts.
- Cardinal's creative audit: our strongest existing asset type is natural, UGC-style
  PT-led storytelling — this series is that, on purpose.
