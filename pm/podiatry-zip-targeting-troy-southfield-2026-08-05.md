## 2. Why these ZIPs — the demand evidence

**Troy has the strongest explicit-city podiatry demand in the whole footprint, and we
serve none of it.**

- **Semrush (US):** "podiatrist troy mi" ≈ **210 searches/mo** — more than Southfield
  (110), Rochester Hills (140), Farmington Hills (90), Sterling Heights (20), Royal Oak
  (20). CPC ~$2.75, competition 0.69 — contested, consistent with Joe's start-small call.
- **The bigger pool is "near me":** podiatrist near me ~33,100/mo national, foot & ankle
  specialist near me 2,900, ankle doctor near me 1,300, plantar fasciitis treatment near
  me 880, diabetic foot doctor near me 480, bunion doctor near me 390. These distribute
  by population — radius/ZIP targeting is exactly how we capture the Troy-ring share.
- **GA4 (90d, MI only):** foot/ankle pages already draw Troy 18 sessions / 37 key
  events, Royal Oak 25, Rochester Hills 4, Sterling Heights 5, Madison Heights 4 —
  with **zero podiatry ad spend aimed at Troy** (the only Troy sessions in the podiatry
  campaigns are geo bleed). Demand leaks in on its own; we've never bid on it.
- **GSC (90d):** SHP has **no visibility for Troy-modified podiatry queries** (zero
  rows), while ranking pos ~3–5 for the same terms in Southfield/Farmington Hills where
  clinics + GBP listings exist. Organic will lag until a Troy foot & ankle page/listing
  exists → **paid is the immediate Troy lever.**
- **Name demand is real:** "dr sorensen podiatrist" ~150 impressions/90d (pos ~7–9);
  "dr leff podiatrist" 198 impressions, pos 1.3, 34 clicks. Cheap exact-match name terms
  in the Doctors/Branded campaign protect this as Troy awareness grows.
- **Line context (internal, Jan–Jul 2026):** Foot & Ankle = 1,042 new patients, **12%
  B2B — the most consumer-driven line SHP has** (+114% growth). Paid search is the right
  tool for this line, more than any other.
- **Competition check:** GSC shows branded queries for "Foot & Ankle Specialists of
  Southeast Michigan – Troy/Sterling" — competitors are established in the Troy ring
  (supports start-small + tight ZIP control rather than a wide blast).

**Capacity sanity check (assumption, for the demand-vs-capacity question):** if a full
podiatry clinic day runs ~20–30 visits with roughly a third new patients, two Troy days
need ~13–20 NPs/week. Troy-modified search (~210/mo) plus the Troy-ring share of
"near me" volume is a demand pool multiples of that — the constraint will be impression
share/budget and conversion path, not raw demand. Blue Ox to validate with Ads-side
volume + impression-share data per the 8/4 ask.

## 3. Mechanics preferences (from the Cardinal audit follow-ups — Blue Ox to confirm)

1. **ZIP lists, not radius, as the implementation** — radius is fine as the mental
   model (primary ≈ a 5–7 mi ring), but ZIP lists are what make the no-overlap rule
   auditable across the three podiatry pods. **Presence-only targeting** ("people in or
   regularly in"), not "interest."
2. **Ad schedule:** weight to Dr. Sorensen's actual Troy clinic days once the 2-day
   schedule is fixed (avoid paying for demand on days we can't book quickly).
3. **Location asset:** Troy clinic (5107 Rochester Rd) on the Troy pod; keep Southfield
   assets off it.
4. **Landing pages, immediate vs development:** launch with
   `/specialty/foot-ankle-specialists/` (1,248 views/90d, the strongest foot page) and/or
   the provider bios (Sorensen bio: 358 views/90d). A true Troy podiatry LP (location +
   provider + conditions, no full nav) goes in the **development** bucket — Cardinal
   flagged the site has no true paid LPs. Any new LP copy is draft-pending-clinical-review
   per our standard guardrails.
5. **Keywords:** the Cardinal foot/ankle ad-group buildout (12 groups incl. a dedicated
   "podiatrist" group — currently missing) applies to the Troy pod from day one; add
   exact-match "dr sorensen podiatrist" / "dr leff podiatrist" name terms cheaply.
6. **What we'd like back from Blue Ox** (matches the 8/4 email): current geo sets of
   both podiatry campaigns; est. volume + impression share for the primary ZIP list;
   incremental budget rec; expected incremental NPs/week (Liine-qualified, not intent
   clicks).
