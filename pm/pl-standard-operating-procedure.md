## 3. The visit standard (the 90-second frame)

**THANK → OPEN → MESSAGE → ASK.** Per-account wording is pre-loaded on every stop card in the app.

- **THANK** — *"Thank you for trusting us with the care of your patients."* No counts, no "this
  year." Individual thanks are given discreetly and in private with the referring provider only.
- **OPEN** — a service question: *"What could we do better — access, notes back, anything?"* Listen.
- **MESSAGE** — one sentence, matched to practice type (per `pm/pl-visit-messages.md` §2).
- **ASK** — one behavior: protect = *"anything pending we can get seen this week?"* · convert =
  *"make us your default"* · cross-sell = *"send the next back/neck the way you send knees"* ·
  prospect = *"send us a handful — let us show you what we're about."*
- **Leave-behind** from the rotating set (see §7 Materials) + coordinator card + direct line.

## 5. Payer & scheduling rules (protects every promise we make)

- **WC / auto no-fault:** no referral, no copay — schedule directly. Attorney relationships are
  **service-only** (records turnaround, fast evaluations); never inducements of any kind.
- **HMO plans (BCN, HAP, McLaren, Priority HMO):** PCP referral required — offer to handle the
  paperwork with the practice's staff.
- **Medicaid-likely flags (red chip):** do **not** promise same-week access until intake verifies;
  select providers only. Uninsured → Harmony Health Direct Pay.
- When unsure, the line is: *"Intake will confirm coverage the same day."*
- Patients we can't serve (excluded body regions, non-covered plans): capture the reason; the
  partner-referral routing list (call-center "treatment not offered" analysis) is the pending fix.

## 8. Measurement & review rhythm

- **Daily:** check-ins logged same day (unlogged work doesn't exist in the data).
- **Monday:** huddle — radar, week plan, promote/hold/park calls.
- **Friday:** exports in; scorecard published.
- **Monthly:** leadership review vs the ramp. **The measurement contract:** weeks 1–2 are judged on
  activity (lapses → 0, visit-days on plan); the patient line reads from mid-August (31-day median
  lag); **September is the verdict month.** August will look soft — the June–July activity dip is
  already baked in; no re-planning on a flat August patient line.

## 9. Compliance (blocking; not aspirational)

No payments, gifts, or inducements for referrals — ever (AKS/Stark). No PHI in writing, notes,
texts, or within earshot of anyone; discreet private thanks with the referring provider are
permitted. No superiority or outcome claims — verified claims only (same-week access, integrated
ortho+spine model, 96% recommend). No access promises to unverified panels. All clinical or
attorney-facing collateral passes compliance review before use.
