## Part 3 — The patient conversation ⭐ (~10 min — the most valuable part)

Answer these for your **#1 procedure from Part 2**. If you have energy for a second
procedure, repeat the exercise — but one is genuinely enough to start. These answers become
the question-and-answer pages patients find when they search.

11. ⭐ What are the **five questions patients ask you most** in clinic about this procedure?
12. ⭐ What's the **biggest misconception** patients arrive with?
13. ⭐ Who is a **good candidate** for this surgery — and who should *not* have it (or not yet)?
14. What should a patient usually have tried **before** surgery is on the table?
15. ⭐ **Recovery, in real-life terms.** Roughly when can a typical patient:
    put weight on the foot · drive · go back to a desk job · go back to a job on their feet ·
    wear regular shoes · exercise again? (Ranges are fine — we'll always add that every
    patient is different and this isn't individual medical advice.)
16. What does the day of surgery look like? (Type of anesthesia, roughly how long, do they
    go home the same day?)
17. Pain: what do you honestly tell patients to expect, and how do you manage it?
18. What warning signs after surgery mean "call us right away"?
19. Is there a sentence you find yourself repeating to almost every one of these patients?
    Give it to us word-for-word — this is what makes the page sound like you and not like
    every other medical website.
