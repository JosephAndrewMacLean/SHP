## 3. One tune-up, for everyone (Coty's old notes are the template)

On busy weeks the note shrinks to a stamp — this week a lot of entries read just *"immediate
spine availability."* The stamp tells us we said our line; it doesn't tell us anything we can
use in three weeks. The fix is one line, ten seconds, dictated in the car:

> **WHO you spoke to → anything NEW → NEXT step.**
> *"Spoke w/ Sue — no referral issues, just low volume; provider only in afternoons, no
> lunches → next: catch Dr. after 1pm with Saylor card."*

That's it. No essays — one line protects the relationship memory if a route ever changes hands
(we just lived through this with the Sean handoff).
