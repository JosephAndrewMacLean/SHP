## 1. Purpose (one paragraph)

Spine, Diagnostic Imaging (MRI), and Chronic Pain Management (internally: Pain Management) are
**one patient journey split across three schedules** — a back-pain caller may need a pain consult
first, an MRI second, and a surgeon third. Today the call center has a drafted script for **spine
only**, scheduling is **fragmented across NextGen / OrthoPlex / hybrid** (clinical via Kelly,
surgical via Anna, imaging separately), and imaging even has **separate insurance participation**
from the practice. This session puts the three service lines and the call center in one room to
agree on **one front-door protocol: how each call type is handled, qualified, routed, tagged, and
scheduled — and how the information (orders, prior imaging, auth status, referral source) flows
between the three lines without falling on the floor.**

## 2. Why now — the data forcing this

- **Spine is at ~46–48/week vs. a 65 floor (Aug 30) and 72 goal (Sept 30).** Qualification +
  routing at intake is Lever A of the 90-day plan (**+6 to +9 NPs/week**, no new spend).
- **Demand already arrives and doesn't convert:** spine landing-page visits +44%, yet NPs/day
  barely moved (6.8→7.2). The drop-off is at the phone/scheduling layer — this meeting's layer.
- **166 canceled/no-show patients self-rescheduled online and kept** — proof of phone/access
  friction. Cross-line handoffs (pain→imaging→surgeon) are where patients leak today.
- **Pain Management is declining** (292 NPs Jan–Jul, worst-trending line) while it is also the
  **conservative front door that keeps spine patients in-system** — how pain calls are tagged and
  scheduled directly moves (or hides) the spine number.
- **Imaging is a revenue line, a spine feeder, AND a referral-intelligence source** (external MRI
  orders name the referring provider) — but facility payer participation is **separate** and
  verified by no one at intake today.
- **The intake script v1.0 is drafted but blocked** on clinical sign-offs (red-flag escalation
  path, surgical routing, Salar redirect). This meeting is the venue to close them.
- Rep training and go-live are scheduled **Jul 28–Aug 4** — decisions must land first, or we
  train the team twice.

### 6A. One front door, three doors behind it

Every new-patient call gets the same skeleton — **greet → reason → insurance pre-screen →
red-flag screen (spine/pain callers) → route → soonest real slot → capture source → confirm** —
then branches by line:

```
                        ┌─ CALLER SAYS… ─────────────────────────────┐
                        │                                             │
        "neck/back pain, sciatica,        "chronic pain,        "I need an MRI /
         maybe surgery"                    injections,           my doctor ordered
                │                          flare-up"             a scan"
                ▼                              ▼                       ▼
        SPINE INTAKE (script v1.0)      PAIN INTAKE (new)      IMAGING INTAKE (new)
        red-flag screen → door sort:    red-flag screen if     order in hand? →
        conservative/interventional     spine-adjacent →       verify FACILITY
        (Oddo·Lee·Kassa·Singh) vs       route to pain team     payer participation →
        surgical (McCarty·Maslak·       (Oddo·Lee·Kassa·       schedule modality →
        Varghese) per approved rubric   Singh) → tag right     no order? → offer eval
                │                       line (see 6C!)         visit — route, don't refuse
                ▼                              ▼                       ▼
        └───────────── every booking tagged: line · door · payer path · source ────────────┘
                          then confirmations, prep instructions, recovery queue
```

**Rules that hold across all three lines (from v1.0, unchanged):**
- Insurance pre-screen **before** booking; Medicaid = **verify per provider**, never a flat no;
  uninsured/non-covered → **Harmony Health Direct Pay + financing**, never a dead end.
- HMO plans (BCN, HAP, McLaren, Priority Health HMO, BCN Advantage) need a PCP referral →
  book **referral-pending**, don't lose the slot.
- Auto No-Fault / Workers' Comp: accepted, no referral, capture claim details — high-value for
  all three lines.
- Red-flag screen for any spine/pain caller; any YES → urgent escalation path, **never** a
  routine future slot.
- Soonest **real** slot only. Same-week is the differentiator — offer it when the system shows
  it, never as a blanket promise.
- "How did you hear about us?" — **required on 100% of calls, all three lines.**

### 6B. Imaging calls — proposed handling rules (NEW — the room's main gap)

1. **Order in hand (internal SHP physician):** verify the order covers the modality/body part →
   verify **facility** payer participation (imaging participation is separate from practice
   participation — Pure Open MRI / Instant Imaging ⚠️ confirm current entity names) → check
   prior-auth status (SHP handles auth post-consult, 3–7 business days — schedule *after* auth
   or flag as auth-pending per Greg's guidance) → book → prep instructions (openness/claustro
   options, contrast, arrival time).
2. **Order in hand (external physician):** same as above **plus capture the ordering provider
   and practice name** — this is referral-engine gold; it feeds Map My Customer so the liaisons
   see which outside physicians already trust us. Results go back to the orderer fast (SLA — D9)
   because turnaround is *the* reason an outside physician keeps ordering here.
3. **No order ("I think I need an MRI"):** we cannot scan without an order ⚠️ (confirm with
   imaging/RCM) — so **route, don't refuse**: "The fastest way to get the right scan is a quick
   evaluation — I can get you in with our [spine/pain/ortho] team, often same week, and if
   imaging's needed it happens right here under one roof." That converts an imaging inquiry into
   a qualified NP instead of a hang-up.
4. **Prior imaging exists elsewhere:** capture where/when (minimum necessary), remind the patient
   to bring images/report — don't re-scan by default; the physician decides.
5. **Known issue:** the Troy "Oakland MRI" Maps misdirect sends confused imaging calls our way
   (Paul owns the GBP fix). Until fixed, reps handle these callers per rule 1–3 — never "wrong
   number" them.

### 6C. Pain Management calls — proposed handling rules (NEW)

1. **Same skeleton, pain branch:** insurance pre-screen → red-flag screen when spine-adjacent
   (neck/back/radiating symptoms) → book to the interventional team (Oddo · Lee · Kassa · Singh)
   at the nearest location.
2. **The tagging question that moves the spine number (decision D7):** a neck/back caller booked
   with a pain physician is — per the spine script's qualified-booking definition — **Service
   line = Spine, Door = Conservative-Interventional**, *not* "Pain." Non-spine chronic pain
   (e.g., joint pain, neuropathy) tags **Pain**. If the room doesn't nail this rule, the spine
   scorecard undercounts and Pain's decline is overstated. Santosh confirms the scorecard reads
   the tag, not the physician's department.
3. **Injection/procedure scheduling:** consult first vs. direct-to-procedure for established
   pathways; prior-auth lead times per payer (Dr. Oddo + Greg bring reality). Never book a
   procedure slot auth-pending unless that's the approved workflow.
4. **Escalation both directions:** pain team flags surgical candidates → warm handoff to the
   surgical door **without re-intake** (D8); surgeons send non-surgical patients back to pain —
   same rule. The patient never re-enters the phone queue to move between our own doors.

### 6D. The scheduling matrix (the "who books what, where" the room must ratify)

| Call / booking type | Handled first by | Booked by / in ⚠️confirm | Slot rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| New spine — conservative/interventional | Call center (script v1.0) | Kelly's team → NextGen (clinical) | Soonest real; protected same-week spine slots when available |
| New spine — surgical-intent consult | Call center (script v1.0) | Kelly's team → NextGen (clinical consult) | Soonest real with the approved surgical-door physician |
| Surgery itself (post-consult) | Surgeon's team | **Anna** (surgical schedule) | Clinical decision; not a call-center booking |
| New pain (spine-adjacent) | Call center (pain branch) | Kelly's team → NextGen · **tag = Spine / Conservative** | Same as spine conservative |
| New pain (non-spine) | Call center (pain branch) | Kelly's team → NextGen · tag = Pain | Soonest real |
| Injection / procedure | Pain team post-consult | ⚠️ pain team vs. central — decide (D6) | After auth clears, per approved workflow |
| Imaging — internal order | Call center or clinic desk ⚠️ | Imaging scheduler (system ⚠️ — Santosh maps) | After facility-payer verify; auth-pending flagged |
| Imaging — external order | Call center | Imaging scheduler + **MMC source capture** | Same + results-back SLA to orderer (D9) |
| Imaging — no order | Call center | Convert to eval visit (spine/pain/ortho) | Route, don't refuse (D5) |
| Red-flag caller (spine or pain) | Call center | **No routine booking** — urgent escalation path (D1) | Immediate warm transfer / 911-ER guidance |
| Established follow-up (any line) | Call center | Existing provider's schedule | Per provider protocol |
| HMO referral-pending (any line) | Call center | Book + `referral-pending` flag | Confirm referral before visit; worklist owns the chase |
| Canceled/no-show (any line) | Recovery queue (CC-B.1) | Daily re-book worklist | Include pain + imaging, not just spine |

**Every row logs the same tag set:** `Service line` · `Door` (spine only) · `Payer path` ·
`Source` (+ referring/ordering practice name) · `Qualified booking Y/N` · imaging adds
`Order status: in-hand / pending / none→eval-offered` and `Auth status`.

### 6E. Information flow between the three lines (what today falls on the floor)

- **Intake → clinical:** the tag set + minimum-necessary reason for visit travels with the
  booking. No detailed histories collected by phone (HIPAA minimum-necessary).
- **Imaging → spine/pain physician:** results routed to the ordering physician within the SLA
  (D9); surgical-intent consults should be **decision-ready** — imaging done or in hand before
  the consult when an order exists (D10 — clinical call, big lever on "orders written").
- **External orders → Map My Customer:** ordering-practice names flow to the liaison CRM so PLs
  stop "spraying and praying" — and the **referring-physician field is never overwritten by
  billing** (change-log fix already on Santosh's list, ATTR workstream).
- **Between our own doors:** pain↔surgical↔imaging handoffs are warm transfers on one record —
  the patient repeats nothing, no new-patient re-intake, no second phone queue (D8).
- **Liine:** all three lines' calls tracked; conversion tracking is **non-functional pending a
  compliance fix** (Paul) — no paid-media conclusions from call data until validated.

## 11. Compliance guardrails on everything above

- **HIPAA minimum-necessary** at intake; anonymized patterns only in the meeting; recording
  consent per policy.
- **Reps never triage clinically** — they read approved questions and route; anything
  ambiguous escalates. Red flag = never a routine booking.
- **Money statements accurate** — no coverage promises, no unverified quotes; Medicaid varies
  by provider (verify, don't decline); Direct Pay described exactly as it works.
- **No over-promising access** — "soonest real slot," same-week only when the system shows it.
- **Results/turnaround claims** (D9) stay internal until validated — nothing promised to
  external physicians we can't consistently deliver.
- This package and all outputs are **drafts pending human clinical/ops review** — nothing here
  is cleared policy until Katie + clinical sign.
