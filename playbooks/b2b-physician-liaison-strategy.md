## The 6 strategy pillars

### 1. Build the tiered target-account universe (it's currently empty)
- One shared, **practice-level** target-account list (not individual providers) across spine feeders:
  **orthopedic practices, chiropractors, pain management, urgent care, PCPs, physical therapy, PM&R,
  ERs, and workers-comp / personal-injury attorneys** (mirror the attorney-referral model already used).
- **Tier 1/2/3** by qualified spine-NP potential (historical + 90-day yield, proximity, payer fit).
- **Double the active spine accounts** and **double spine field time**, accepting reduced ortho-account
  coverage during the test — a decision already agreed by leadership.

### 2. Territory & assignment (kill overlap)
- Assign each account a single owner with **geographic ownership** across Livonia, Sterling Heights,
  Southfield, Troy, Port Huron — eliminate the location overlap the paid-media audit flagged.
- Weight coverage to **Sterling Heights (defend vs. Corewell/Beaumont)** and the **Southfield/Troy ramp**;
  Jasmine's Livonia/ortho-heavy territory rebalanced toward spine feeders.

### 3. Qualified-yield productivity (measure outcomes, not activity)
- Primary KPI: **qualified spine NPs per PL per week** and **NP per 100 practice visits** — not raw visits/touches.
- **Cody must sustain 15 NP/week** before the 4th PL is hired; use that as the productivity bar.
- Weekly ride-alongs + capture the top producer's playbook (what she says, leaves behind, follows up on)
  into a repeatable script the whole team runs.

### 4. Referral loop & frictionless referring (what makes them keep sending)
- **Same-week access reserved for referrals** — the pitch to every practice.
- **Closed-loop report-back** to the referring provider after the visit (seen, plan) — the single
  biggest driver of repeat referrals; automate it as tracking allows.
- Make referring effortless: a dedicated line/fax/secure path, a named coordinator, and a **referrer
  one-pager** (deep spine bench, conservative-to-surgical pathway, how/where to refer).
- Route referred **surgical** candidates to the **higher-converting spine surgeons** (spine plan §4A).

### 5. Attribution fix (stop flying blind)
- Clean **Map My Customer ↔ NextGen** crosswalk so each referral ties to a source practice.
- Stop the **referring-physician field being overwritten by billing**; add a change log; require the
  call center to capture referral source at intake (see call-center strategy §3).
- Retire the manual 6–8 hrs/week of call-listening once source is captured cleanly.

### 6. Cadence, OODA & hiring
- Tiered **visit cadence** per account; weekly review with **1/2/3/4-week OODA loops** after each change.
- **Drop persistently zero-yield accounts** after validation; double down on winners.
- **4th-PL hire trigger:** Cody sustaining 15 NP/week + a validated Tier-1 backlog; onboard to the top-producer playbook.

## Guardrails
No PHI in referral comms without authorization · accurate representation of physicians/credentials ·
no inducements that violate anti-kickback / Stark (referral relationships are on clinical merit and
service, never payment) · HIPAA-compliant closed-loop communication.
