### How the Ideas Bank feeds the machine
1. Score every new idea Impact/Effort/Cost → the sheet auto-assigns a **Quadrant**.
2. In the **Wednesday** check-in, promote 1–2 **Quick Wins** into **Master Tasks** (set *Linked Task*).
3. Track them to Done on the **Dashboard** like any other task. Big Bets get a start date and an owner,
   never an open-ended "someday." This is the loop that keeps "what works" continuously converting into shipped work.
