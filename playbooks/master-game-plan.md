## 1. North star & the honest strategy
**Protect the business by hitting qualified spine volume — 72/week by Sept 30 — while ortho
over-delivers to carry the shortfall.** This is leadership's agreed posture, and it's realistic:

- **Ortho = the compensating engine:** hold **130–140 new patients/week (~120–125% of surgery
  target)** as the new standard. Ortho already turned around 75%→125% of plan in ~60 days — the model.
- **Spine = the fix-in-progress:** climb from ~46–48/week to the **65 floor**, then **72/week**, via
  **qualified** patients — right candidate, right insurance, routed to the right (converting) surgeon —
  not raw volume (which already failed to move the number).
- **Marketing owns qualified demand + conversion plumbing; it does NOT own surgical conversion.**
  3 of 4 spine surgeons miss conversion ratios and Salar absorbs ~21% of spine NPs at ~3% conversion —
  a **clinical/ops dependency (Katie + physicians)** named here so the right team owns it.

**Why Sept 30 is hard:** ASC staffing has already been cut; further spine staffing (incl. reducing to
3 spine surgeons) is tied to hitting the number. This is existential, not aspirational.

## 5. Cadence & governance
- **Mon / Wed / Fri** leadership rhythm; **daily 4pm** access check-in until capacity is stable.
- One **weekly scorecard** rolling up all five workstreams: separate **activity** (accounts, calls,
  pages), **output** (qualified + total spine NPs, ortho NPs), **efficiency** (NP/100 visits, CPA,
  qualified-booking rate). Review 1/2/3/4 weeks after each change (OODA).

## 6. Cross-cutting risks
- **Surgical conversion doesn't improve** → named leadership dependency; report marketing's qualified-NP
  number separately from ASC conversion so problems aren't conflated.
- **Line tracking stays broken** → Week-1 blocker; no paid-bidding changes until fixed (B2C CPA already blew up $200→$2,000).
- **Structural constraints** (no Medicaid, no hospital affiliation, no portal, Epic leakage) → qualify at
  intake; lean on **Harmony Health Direct Pay** for non-covered patients.
- **Target conflict:** working figure **317/mo (72/wk)** vs. budget doc **321** — reconcile with Santosh.
- **Rebrand loose ends** (Mendelson legacy links, Troy Maps/"Oakland MRI" misdirects) → close so paid/organic/referral traffic lands.

## 7. Open decisions for leadership
1. Confirm the **spine target (317 vs. 321)** and the ortho **130–140/week** standard.
2. Approve **routing surgical candidates away from low-conversion clinic time** (clinical call).
3. Green-light the **4th PL** trigger criteria and the **AI call-center** rollout timing.
4. Own the **surgical-conversion** workstream (Katie + physicians) as a tracked dependency.
