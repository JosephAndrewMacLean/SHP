## 3. Page standard (applies to every page in this roadmap)

The Cardinal-aligned template — this is what "rebuilt" means below:

- **One canonical URL** per topic; 301 all duplicates/legacy variants to it.
- **Definition-first opening** (2–3 plain sentences answering the query), then a **Quick
  Answer box** — the structures AI Overviews and featured snippets extract.
- **12+ patient FAQs** — sourced from Dr. Sorensen's questionnaire Part 3, in his phrasing.
- **Candidacy section** (who this surgery is for / who it isn't) + **conservative-first
  pathway** (what to try before surgery — credibility with patients and E-E-A-T).
- **Recovery milestone table** in real-life terms (weight-bearing, driving, desk job,
  standing job, regular shoes, exercise).
- **When to seek care now** (red-flag list).
- **"Medically reviewed by Kevin Sorensen, DPM"** byline + review date (pending his Part 5
  consent) and provider module linking his bio.
- **Local module:** Troy-priority topics carry the Troy office block (5107 Rochester Rd,
  MRI + PT at 5119 Rochester Rd), same-week access, 96% recommend rate.
- **Grade 6–8 reading level**, WCAG AA, educational disclaimer kept visually separate from
  any statistic (FTC), no outcome promises.
- **Schema-ready structure** (FAQPage + MedicalProcedure/MedicalCondition, Physician link):
  we write the content blocks; schema deployment is Cardinal's 90-day lane — hand off, don't
  duplicate (0 FAQ rich results sitewide today = the tripwire that proves it landed).
