# Spine Patient Acquisition — Process Playbook

**Goal:** a repeatable process that consistently brings *new* spine patients into the
SHP practice, converts inquiries into booked consults, and shows up in the same door.

> This is a process, not a one-off campaign. Run it continuously; the metrics tell you
> which engine to feed. Anything patient-facing or clinical here is a **draft pending
> medical/legal review** (see §7). Items marked `[CONFIRM]` need a real SHP fact.

---

## 0. Why spine is different

New spine patients arrive through **two engines that must run in parallel**:

- **Engine A — Consumer / self-referral.** People with back or neck pain searching
  online, asking AI assistants, or hearing about you locally. High volume, high
  anxiety, long consideration, strong "near me" intent.
- **Engine B — Professional referral network.** For spine especially, a large share
  of high-value patients are *referred*: primary care, physical therapists,
  chiropractors, pain management, ER/urgent care, and (where relevant) workers' comp
  and personal-injury attorneys. This engine is relationship-driven, not search-driven.

Most practices over-invest in A and neglect B, or win the inquiry and then **lose the
patient at intake** (slow callback, no online scheduling, insurance friction). This
process addresses all three: demand, referrals, and conversion.

## 1. The spine patient journey (the funnel we're optimizing)

```
                 ┌──────────────── ENGINE A: CONSUMER ────────────────┐
  Pain onset ──▶ Research ──▶ Discover SHP ──▶ Inquiry ──▶ Booked ──▶ Show ──▶ Patient
                 (search/AI)   (site/GBP/PR)   (call/form)  consult     up      (+ reviews,
                                                                                 +referrals back)
                 └──────────────── ENGINE B: REFERRAL ────────────────┘
  PCP/PT/chiro/pain/ER sees patient ──▶ chooses where to send ──▶ referral to SHP ──▶ Booked ──▶ Show ──▶ Patient
```

We optimize every arrow. A missing arrow (e.g. "Booked → Show" no-shows, or referral
sent but never scheduled) leaks patients no matter how much demand we create.

## 2. The acquisition process, step by step (with owners)

### Stage 1 — Be findable & credible (owners: content-creator, seo, aeo, geo, aio)
The moment someone types "back pain specialist near me," asks an AI "should I see a
spine doctor for sciatica," or a PCP Googles you before referring — SHP must appear,
and look like the trustworthy expert choice.

- **Spine content cluster** (content-creator): a pillar page ("Spine & Back Pain Care
  at SHP") plus supporting articles on the questions real patients ask —
  *sciatica, herniated disc, spinal stenosis, "do I need spine surgery?", non-surgical
  options, what to expect at your first spine visit, recovery timelines, is it covered.*
  Each with a named, credentialed spine physician author + reviewer and a review date.
- **Search + local** (seo): spine service page, per-location pages, schema
  (`Physician`, `MedicalClinic`, `FAQPage`), Google Business Profile optimized for
  spine ("orthopedic spine surgeon," "back pain," photos, hours, Q&A), consistent NAP.
- **Answer/AI surfaces** (aeo/aio/geo): concise answer blocks + FAQ schema for
  snippet/voice/AI-Overview capture; format content to be citable by ChatGPT/Perplexity/
  Gemini; monthly prompt-test of target spine queries to see where SHP is/isn't surfaced.
- **Trust signals everywhere:** surgeon credentials, conservative-care-first philosophy
  (reduces surgery fear), honest reviews, insurance/coverage clarity.

### Stage 2 — Build the referral engine (owners: pr-specialist + practice leadership)
This is the highest-leverage, most-neglected engine for spine.

- **Map & prioritize referrers** `[CONFIRM]`: list local PCPs, PT clinics,
  chiropractors, pain-management and urgent-care/ER sources within the catchment;
  rank by volume potential and current relationship.
- **Referrer-facing capabilities one-pager:** conditions treated, conservative vs.
  surgical approach, *how fast a referred patient will be seen*, how to refer, and who
  to call. Make referring frictionless.
- **Make referring easy:** a simple referral pathway (secure fax/portal/dedicated line
  `[CONFIRM channel]`), a named liaison/coordinator, and — critically — **closed-loop
  communication** back to the referrer after the visit (referrers keep sending patients
  to practices that keep them informed).
- **Relationship cadence:** physician-to-physician intros, lunch-and-learns, a quarterly
  update. Owned by leadership/liaison; PR supports with materials and thought leadership.

### Stage 3 — Capture the inquiry (owners: seo/content + intake process)
Turn interest into a contact without friction.

- **One obvious action per page:** "Request a spine consult" — phone (click-to-call on
  mobile), a short form, and **online self-scheduling** if available `[CONFIRM]`.
- **Reduce anxiety at the CTA:** "no referral needed" (if true `[CONFIRM]`), "most
  insurance accepted" (if true `[CONFIRM]`), "start with non-surgical options."
- **Track the source** of every inquiry (call-tracking numbers + form UTM/source tags)
  so we know which engine and which content produced it.

### Stage 4 — Convert to a booked, kept appointment (owner: intake/front desk + director)
This is where most spine leads are won or lost.

- **Speed-to-lead:** respond to every inquiry fast — ideally same business hour.
  Every hour of delay drops conversion. Define an SLA (e.g. **callback < 1 hour**,
  after-hours next-morning) `[CONFIRM current practice]`.
- **Intake script + triage:** a warm, consistent script; capture symptoms, urgency,
  insurance; route to the right provider; offer the soonest reasonable slot.
- **Insurance/coverage handling** up front to avoid drop-off and no-shows.
- **Reminder + no-show reduction:** confirmations and reminders; easy reschedule;
  waitlist to fill cancellations. Track and attack the no-show rate.

### Stage 5 — Turn patients into more patients (owners: content, guerilla, pr)
- **Reviews process:** invite satisfied patients (consented, FTC-compliant) to review —
  reviews drive both local search and consumer trust for the *next* spine patient.
- **Community presence** (guerilla): free "back health / move better" workshops at
  senior centers, gyms, employers, and rec leagues; posture/ergonomics screenings at
  local events; partnerships with running clubs and workplaces with manual labor.
  Low cost, high trust, and it feeds PR and content with real local moments.
- **Earned media** (pr): a true, approved news hook `[CONFIRM]` (new spine program,
  minimally invasive capability, new surgeon, accreditation) + position the spine
  surgeon as the local media expert for back-health topics.

## 4. 90-day rollout (then it runs continuously)

- **Month 1 — Foundation & referral mapping.** Build the spine content cluster (draft →
  medical/legal review); technical + local SEO and GBP; map and prioritize referrers;
  draft the referrer one-pager and referral pathway; stand up call-tracking + source
  tagging; define the intake SLA and script; capture the baseline.
- **Month 2 — Launch demand + referral outreach.** Publish reviewed spine pages with
  answer/FAQ schema; begin referrer outreach and physician intros; turn on
  speed-to-lead intake process; first AI/prompt-test baseline; run community workshop #1.
- **Month 3 — Amplify & tighten conversion.** Earned media on the approved hook;
  reviews process live; attack no-shows; re-run AI/AI-Overview checks; PT/chiro/PCP
  closed-loop reporting in place; 90-day review and decision on scaling.

## 5. Measurement — the acquisition scorecard

Track the whole funnel, by engine, so you fix the actual bottleneck:

- **Demand:** non-branded organic sessions to spine pages; GBP calls/directions;
  AI-citation log for target spine queries.
- **Referrals:** # referring sources active, # referrals received, referral → booked rate.
- **Inquiries:** spine inquiries by source (call vs. form vs. self-schedule).
- **Conversion (the leak detectors):** inquiry → booked rate; **speed-to-lead time**;
  no-show rate; booked → kept (arrived) rate.
- **Outcome (primary KPI):** **new spine patients seen per month**, and cost/effort per
  new patient by channel. `[CONFIRM baseline]`
- **Diagnosis rule:** low patients + high demand → fix conversion/intake. Low patients +
  low demand → fix findability + referrals. Don't buy more demand to paper over a leak.

## 7. Compliance gates (blocking — nothing ships without sign-off)

- All clinical/educational spine content reviewed by a credentialed physician (E-E-A-T/YMYL).
- No PHI; any patient story/testimonial/photo requires signed HIPAA authorization and is
  real, typical, and disclosed (FTC).
- No outcome, "best," "#1," or surgery-success guarantees; conservative, honest claims only.
- Provider credentials/specialties verified, not overstated.
- Accessibility (Section 1557 / ADA / WCAG AA), reading level ~8th grade.
- Referral communications must respect patient privacy and consent.
