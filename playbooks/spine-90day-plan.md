# Spine Realistic Game Plan — clear the 65 floor by Aug 30, operate at 72/week by Sept 30

**Owner:** Joe (marketing/ops) · **Sponsor:** Gautam (CEO) · **PL lead:** Kristen · **Ops/ASC:** Katie
**Window:** Jul 16 – Sep 30, 2026 · **Status:** operating plan, draft pending sign-off
**Grounded in:** `brand/current-state.md`, `brand/provider-roster-by-service-line.md`, the four
internal datasets, and the Jun 29–Jul 16 leadership meeting transcripts.

> This supersedes the earlier volume-first draft. It is deliberately **realistic**: it targets
> **qualified** patients (right clinical candidate, right insurance, routed to the right surgeon),
> not raw lead volume — because the data shows volume alone has already failed to move the number.

---

## 0. The stakes (why Sept 30 is a hard line)
Spine is tracking **~46–48/week vs. a 65 floor and a 72 goal** (~65% of target, ~26 behind pace,
~39% of the July plan). The shortfall has **already triggered ASC staffing cuts**, and leadership
has tied **further spine staffing decisions to Sept 30 performance**. This plan has to produce
**real movement by Aug 30 and steady-state 72/week by Sept 30** — and it has to be honest about
what marketing can and cannot deliver, so the right problems get owned by the right people.

---

## 1. The honest diagnosis — three layers, only one of which marketing fully owns

**Layer 1 — There is a real B2C/organic demand hole.** Spine is budgeted **79% B2C+natural / 21%
referral**. In June, B2C ran **−71 vs. budget** while referrals ran **+14 above**. So the gap is on
the consumer/organic side — *not* the referral side.

**Layer 2 — But more volume hasn't converted.** After the Line launch, spine landing-page visits
rose **+44% (26→33/day)** and new-patient-intent CTR went **26%→30%**, yet **new patients/day barely
moved (6.8→7.2)**. Pouring more traffic in has already been tried and did **not** move the number.
Causes: a departed liaison (Sean, ~2–3 NP/week), **insurance/candidate mismatch** after the click,
broken Line conversion tracking, and broader paid targeting pulling **lower-quality** traffic.

**Layer 3 — The binding constraint is qualification + surgical conversion.** **3 of 4 spine
physicians are missing their surgical-conversion ratios**, and "orders written" is the leading
metric that drives the ASC. **Marketing can raise the *quality* of who walks in and route them to
the right surgeon; it cannot make a physician convert.** That clinical-conversion piece is an
**ops/clinical dependency owned by Katie + the physicians**, and this plan names it as such so
marketing isn't scapegoated for a conversion gap it can't close.

**Conclusion:** the realistic play is **qualified volume, not raw volume** — fix the B2C hole *and*
qualify/route every patient so the traffic actually converts, while leadership fixes surgical
conversion in parallel.

plan) and is ~2× the line's budgeted referral share. We commit to a **credible referral lift** and
put the heavier weight on **recovering B2C and qualifying/converting the traffic that already exists.**

## 4. Workstreams (owners from the actual team)

### A. Qualification & routing — *the highest-leverage, most-overlooked lever* — Owner: **Joe + Kristen + Katie**
- **Define a "qualified spine patient":** symptomatic + imaging-appropriate + **accepted insurance**
  (SHP takes no Medicaid — flag mismatches *before* booking to stop the post-click drop).
- **Insurance pre-qualification at intake** (13-person call center, via Kelly) so mismatched leads
  don't consume surgeon slots and inflate "no-show/cancel."
- **Candidate matching → right door:** conservative/interventional (Oddo, Lee, Kassa, Singh) vs.
  surgical (McCarty, Maslak, Varghese, Salar, Munk, Zamorano). Use interventional/conservative as the
  front door that keeps a patient in-system until they're a surgical candidate.
- **Route surgical candidates to the higher-converting surgeons — the data demands it.** Spine new
  patients Jan–Jul 15 2026: **McCarty 480 · Maslak 309 · Salar 303 · Varghese 168 · Munk 113 ·
  Zamorano 51.** **Dr. Salar absorbs ~21% of all spine new patients (303) while converting almost
  none to surgery (~3%)** — that alone is a large block of wasted top-of-funnel. Redirecting Salar's
  *surgical* candidates to McCarty/Maslak/Varghese is a concrete, no-new-spend lever on the ASC number.
  (This is a clinical/ops routing decision — surface it to Katie + leadership, don't act unilaterally.)
- **Deliverable:** a one-page qualification + routing rubric the call center and PLs both use.

### B. B2C / organic demand recovery — *the real −71 hole* — Owner: **Randall + Joe + Cardinal**
- **80% of SEO to spine** (already decided): the 5 condition hubs (stenosis, herniated disc,
  sciatica, DDD, spondylolisthesis), provider pages, FAQ/schema; **2 spine pages this week, 3 next.**
- **Spine differentiation** (currently missing everywhere): tell the deep-bench story — 5 spine
  surgeons incl. a neurosurgeon + 4 interventional-pain physicians + a full conservative-to-surgical
  pathway under one roof. Put it on the pages, the referrer one-pager, and PL leave-behinds.
- **Push booking to the free website path** (75% capture at $0 vs. paid Zocdoc ~52–57%); trim the
  least-efficient paid Zocdoc spine spend.
- **Do NOT scale paid spend on broken tracking** — B2C CPA already blew up $200→$2,000. Fix tracking (D) first.

### C. Referral engine — realistic growth — Owner: **Kristen + Cody + Jasmine**
- **Recover the Sean loss** (~2–3 NP/week) first — that's the fastest referral win.
- **Tiered spine target-account list** (Tier 1/2/3) across ortho, pain, chiro, urgent care, PCP, PT —
  weighted to **Sterling Heights (defend vs. Corewell/Beaumont)** and the **Southfield/Troy ramp**.
  (Cody sustains 15 NP/week before a 4th PL is added.)
- **Measure qualified NP yield per 100 visits, not visits** — coach the team toward the top
  producer's method; don't just add hours.
- **Same-week access reserved for referrals** + closed-loop report-back to referrers.

### D. Attribution & the tracking fix — Owner: **Santosh + Joe + Paul**
- **Fix Line conversion tracking** (currently non-functional on a compliance issue) *before* any
  paid-bidding change — otherwise paid spine dollars are flying blind.
- Fix attribution: **"how did you hear" is asked only 60–70%** of calls; the **referring-physician
  field is overwritten by billing**; **Map My Customer has no NextGen mapping.** Stand up the
  NextGen↔MMC crosswalk so PLs stop "spraying and praying."
- **Stand up and fill the Weekly Spine Scorecard** (activity vs. output vs. efficiency).

### E. Access / conversion plumbing — Owner: **Kelly + Joe**
- Work the **166 canceled/no-show patients who self-rescheduled online** — a phone/access-friction
  signal. Tighten callback SLA and protect same-week spine slots.

## 7. Top risks & how this plan handles them
- **Surgical conversion doesn't improve** → named as a leadership-owned dependency; marketing's
  number is *qualified NPs delivered to the line*, reported separately from ASC conversion so the
  two problems don't get conflated.
- **Line tracking stays broken** → it's a Week-1 blocker; no paid-bidding changes until it's fixed.
- **Insurance/Medicaid structural mismatch** → pre-qualify at intake; lean on the **Harmony Health
  Direct Pay** program for non-covered/uninsured candidates rather than losing them.
- **Target conflict** → working figure **317/month (72/week)**; budget doc says **321** — reconcile with Santosh.
- **Referrals over-promised** → capped at a realistic recover-Sean + tiered-expansion lift, not 150.

## 8. Compliance gates (blocking)
Physician review on clinical/spine content (E-E-A-T/YMYL) · no PHI / no unconsented patient stories
(HIPAA) · substantiate efficacy stats, never adjacent to a disclaimer such that they read as a
guarantee (FTC) · verify surgeon credentials · direct-pay / financing claims accurate · referrer
communications respect patient privacy and consent.
