## 1. The target math (from your own data)

| Metric | June 2026 baseline | Sept 30 target | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total spine NPs / month** | **248** | **317** | **+69** |
| Total spine NPs / week | ~57 | **72** | +15 |
| — Referral / PL (B2B) | 79 | **~150** | **+71** |
| — Direct (B2C) | 169 | 169 (hold) | 0 |
| B2B share of spine | 32% | 47% | +15 pts |

Leadership's plan closes the **entire** +69 through referrals (B2B 79 → 150 — nearly a
double in 11 weeks). That's the right primary lever (spine is your **most referral-dependent
line at 32% B2B**), but putting 100% of the target on one nearly-doubling engine is fragile.

**This plan de-risks it by closing the gap on two fronts** so 72 is robust if referrals land
at, say, 140 instead of 150:

| Lever | Contribution to the +69 | Why it's credible on this timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Referral engine (primary)** | **+55 to +71** | The stated mandate; efficiency + mix + visits (below) |
| **B. Access & capture (fast hedge)** | **+8 to +14** | Fixing phone friction + shifting booking to the free website path — pure conversion, no new demand needed |

If B lands, referrals only need to reach **~135–140** to hit 317 — a materially safer ask than 150.

### The referral capacity math
Q2-2025 baseline productivity = **33.6 new patients per 100 practice visits** (Kristen ~**70**,
most of the team 6–40). To add **+71 spine NPs/month** at the team's blended ~34/100 needs
**~+210 spine-focused practice visits/month (~+50/week)**. But **every point of efficiency you
buy by coaching the team toward Kristen's playbook cuts that visit burden** — at 50/100 the
same lift needs only ~+140 visits/month. So the plan attacks **all three**: mix (shift PL time
to spine), efficiency (coach up NP/100), and volume (more spine visits) — not brute-force visits alone.

## 5. Top risks & mitigations
- **Referrals don't nearly-double in time** → the B2C access/capture hedge (Lever B) covers ~10–14
  of the gap; coaching efficiency lowers the visit burden. Watch NP/100, not just visit count.
- **Concentration on Kristen** → capture and spread her playbook; don't rely on her hours.
- **Attribution not validated** → NextGen↔MMC crosswalk + Liine before any paid-bidding change; treat B2C as inferred.
- **Access promise vs. reality** → *don't market same-week faster than scheduling can deliver* —
  wait-time complaints are the top sentiment risk and would poison both referrals and AIO citations.
- **Scheduling fragmentation (NextGen/OrthoPlex)** → a dependency Santosh is scoping, **not a blocker** to the PL/access push.

## 6. Compliance gates (blocking)
Physician review on any clinical/spine content (E-E-A-T/YMYL) · no PHI / no unconsented patient
stories (HIPAA) · substantiate efficacy stats, never next to a disclaimer such that they read as a
guarantee (FTC) · verify surgeon credentials · referrer communications respect patient privacy/consent.
