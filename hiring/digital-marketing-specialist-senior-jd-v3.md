**Department:** Marketing
**Position:** Digital Marketing Specialist (Senior)
**Supervisory Duties:** None (individual contributor)
**FLSA Status:** Exempt
**Employment Status:** Full-Time
**Reports to:** Senior Director of Marketing

**Summary of Position:**
The Senior Digital Marketing Specialist is the primary execution resource for Synergy Health
Partners' consumer (B2C) digital marketing, with a focus on growing new-patient demand in spine
and orthopedics. Reporting to the Senior Director of Marketing, the role turns strategy and data
into shipped work — search engine optimization (SEO), website and landing-page updates, content,
creative production, and performance reporting. The ideal candidate is performance-oriented,
proactive, and self-directed, works quickly with the help of modern AI tools, and follows the
organization's plain-language and medical-review standards for patient-facing content.

**Job Duties:**
- Execute on-page and technical SEO and build or optimize service-line and condition content to
  improve search rankings, organic traffic, and new-patient conversions.
- Optimize content for AI and answer engines (AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice search).
- Build and update landing pages and improve website performance in WordPress/Elementor;
  maintain analytics and conversion tracking (GA4, Google Tag Manager, UTMs).
- Create in-house graphics and creative assets for web, email, paid ads, and on-site signage.
- Analyze marketing performance and present findings with recommended actions; monitor that
  tracking, ad platforms, and vendor deliverables are functioning.
- Build and deploy email campaigns and coordinate messaging across paid, social, and other
  channels.
- Perform other related duties as assigned.

**Basic Requirements:**
- 2–5 years of hands-on digital marketing experience with demonstrated SEO and
  content-execution results.
- Working knowledge of SEO (on-page, technical, and keyword research), WordPress/Elementor,
  GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and email platforms (e.g., Mailchimp).
- Ability to write clear, accurate copy and produce basic graphic design.
- Comfort pulling, interpreting, and presenting performance data.
- Proficiency with AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) for content, analysis, and workflow
  efficiency.
- Strong organization and the ability to manage multiple priorities with minimal supervision.

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Experience in healthcare or a multi-location business; familiarity with E-E-A-T / YMYL
  standards and medically reviewed content.
- Experience with AI-search / answer-engine optimization.
- Familiarity with CRM or patient-management platforms.
- Interest in marketing operations and process improvement.

**Compensation Details:**
Salary range $75,000–$90,000 annually, commensurate with experience. (Comparable Detroit-market
roles range $72,000–$95,000 in total cash compensation at the 50th–90th percentile.) Eligible
for the organization's full-time benefits per company policy.

**Physical Demands:**
Primarily sedentary office work in a hybrid arrangement based in Troy, MI. Requires extended
periods of computer and screen use and frequent communication by phone and email. Occasional
light lifting (up to 15 lbs). Reasonable accommodations will be made to enable individuals with
disabilities to perform essential functions, consistent with the ADA.
