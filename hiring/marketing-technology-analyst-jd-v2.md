> learnings of the last 3–4 months and scoped to what actually moves B2C spine growth.
> Draft pending leadership (AMP) review and budget approval.

**Team:** B2C Marketing — Synergy Health Partners
**Location:** Hybrid (Troy, MI)
**Reports To:** Director of B2C Marketing
**Salary:** Commensurate with experience
**Target Start:** Upon approval

## Position Summary

Synergy Health Partners is in a hurry-up offense to grow new patients in **spine and
orthopedics**, and the B2C digital front is where much of that opportunity lives — especially
as AI reshapes how patients search for care. We're hiring a **Marketing & Technology Analyst**
to be the execution engine behind that growth.

This person is the **right hand to the Director of B2C Marketing** — the one who turns strategy
into shipped work: SEO and content that ranks and converts, website and landing-page
optimization, creative production, and performance data translated into action. Where leadership
sets direction, this role **executes at the micro level** — page by page, keyword by keyword —
so marketing can move at the speed of the goal.

We're looking for someone **performance-oriented, proactive, and fast** — intellectually curious,
data-fluent, and comfortable using modern AI tools to multiply their output. The right person
brings us finished work and clear recommendations rather than waiting to be told each step.

Patient-facing content is written in plain language (~8th-grade reading level), and anything
clinically substantive routes through medical review before it publishes.

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. SEO & Organic Growth Execution
- Execute on-page and technical SEO: titles/meta, headers, internal linking, schema, and the
  fixes surfaced by our audits and tools (Rank Math, Google Search Console, SEMrush)
- Build and optimize spine and orthopedic condition content to grow topical authority
  (e.g., stenosis, herniated disc, sciatica, DDD, spondylolisthesis)
- Optimize for AI and answer engines (AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice) — meeting patients
  where search is actually going
- Track keyword performance and continuously fine-tune to improve rankings, CTR, and conversion

### 2. Content Creation & Calendar
- Write and optimize patient-facing content — service-line and location pages, condition hubs,
  FAQs, and decision-support content — at an ~8th-grade reading level
- Own the marketing content calendar across service lines, clinic and doctor launches, and
  seasonal themes; keep production moving and on deadline
- Coordinate briefings and asset development with internal teams; route clinically substantive
  content through medical review before publish

### 3. Website Development & Optimization
- Build, update, and optimize landing pages in WordPress + Elementor Pro
- Improve site performance, Core Web Vitals, layout, and functionality; apply basic HTML/CSS/JS
  for quick fixes and enhancements
- Maintain tracking infrastructure (GA4, Google Tag Manager, UTM management, pixels) and protect
  conversion-tracking integrity (e.g., Liine)

### 4. Graphic Design & Creative Production
- Produce in-house graphics and creative assets for web, email, ads, and on-site signage
  (Canva; Adobe Suite a plus)
- Keep creative consistent with the Synergy brand and evolving rebrand standards

### 5. Data → Action & Performance Monitoring
- **Proactively pull, interpret, and present** performance data to the Director — deliver insight
  and recommended next steps, not raw exports
- Track KPIs across channels (rankings, CTR, CPA, conversion, ROAS, new-patient volume) and
  execute the changes the data calls for
- Keep an eye on the whole B2C front so nothing gets missed — confirm tracking/tags are firing,
  ad platforms are healthy, and agency/partner deliverables are on track; flag issues early

### 6. Email & Omnichannel Execution
- Create and deploy email campaigns and nurture automations (Mailchimp or equivalent)
- Coordinate messaging across Google Ads, Meta, SMS, print, and on-site signage
- Manage audience lists, engagement metrics, and deliverability

## What You Bring
- 2–5 years of hands-on digital marketing experience with real SEO and content-execution results
- Working knowledge of:
  - SEO — on-page, technical, and keyword strategy (Rank Math / SEMrush / GSC a plus)
  - WordPress (Elementor preferred)
  - GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, and Meta Ads Manager
  - Mailchimp or similar email-automation platforms
- Content-writing ability — clear, accurate, patient-friendly copy
- Graphic-design capability (Canva; Adobe Suite a plus)
- Data fluency — comfortable pulling, interpreting, and presenting performance data and turning
  it into executed change
- Fluency with AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to generate content, analyze data, and
  accelerate workflows
- **Performance-oriented, proactive, and self-directed** — takes ownership, moves fast, and
  manages multiple priorities without micromanagement

## Bonus Points For
- Healthcare or multi-location business experience; familiarity with E-E-A-T / YMYL standards and
  medically reviewed content
- Experience with AI-search / answer-engine optimization
- Familiarity with CRM or patient-management platforms
- Interest in marketing operations and process improvement

## Why This Role Now
Spine and ortho growth is the priority, and the digital/B2C front is where a lot of the winnable
opportunity sits — particularly with AI changing search. This role is the execution leverage that
lets marketing move at the speed of the goal: someone who can take the strategy and the data and
*go do it*, so the whole function multiplies. You'll have autonomy, real ownership, and a direct
line to the impact of your work — helping more patients across our communities find the care they
need.
