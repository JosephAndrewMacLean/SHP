## Russ's general question — KPIs and how we measure success

The role's job is to close the **B2C spine gap**. June actuals: 248 spine new patients against
a ~305 budget, and the entire miss is on the B2C side (B2C ~169 actual vs ~240 budgeted; B2B
referrals are running above plan). This hire is the execution capacity aimed at that −71/month
hole. I'd hold the role to these numbers:

| KPI | Baseline (today) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Spine B2C new patients** | ~169/mo (Jun) | **+8–11/mo by day 90**, building toward the ~240/mo budget line |
| **Non-branded organic CTR** | 1.15% (vs 3–5% benchmark; 80% of clicks are branded) | **≥2.5% within 6 months** — roughly 2× organic clicks at $0 media cost |
| **Spine content coverage** | 5 core spine condition hubs at near-zero visibility | All 5 published (medically reviewed) and ranking top-10 within 6 months |
| **Cost per captured B2C patient** | Paid Zocdoc ≈ $127–136/captured patient vs website ≈ $0 (website captures 74.7% vs Zocdoc 56.9%) | **Blended cost down 10–15%** by shifting capture to the website/organic path |
| **Speed of implementation** | SEO/content/landing-page fixes queue for weeks | Shipped within ~1 week of identification |
| **Tracking integrity** | Feb outage lost ~10 days of conversion data; B2C CPA spiked ~$200 → ~$2,000 during breakage | Outages caught within 24 hours; no sustained data loss |

**New patients:** the +8–11/month early-window figure is the conservative, directly-attributable
number from our 90-day spine plan for the B2C/organic workstream this hire powers; the full-year
objective is recovering B2C spine to budget (~240/month). **CPA impact:** primarily through mix —
every patient captured via organic/website instead of paid Zocdoc saves ~$127–136. **Google
spend impact:** this is not a promise to cut the budget; it's efficiency on the same spend —
better landing pages lift Quality Score (currently ~4/10 → lower CPC), and faster site/tracking
fixes protect the account from the resets that cost us efficiency in Feb–May. All of this is
measurable in Liine + GA4, and I'll report it monthly.
