    The evidence behind the rules

    why the scoring works the way it does
    • Titles of 40–60 characters earn +33.3% CTR vs titles outside that band — Backlinko, analysis of 4M Google results. That's why the counter turns green there.
    • Pages with a meta description earn ~6% more clicks than pages without one — same Backlinko dataset. Google truncates around 155–160 chars, so the app aims you at 140–155 and accepts 120–158.
    • Clickbait "power words" cut CTR ~14% (Backlinko, 5M results) — and violate healthcare advertising rules anyway. The app warns on hype and hard-blocks "best/#1/guaranteed" (FTC).
    • Question-style titles show no reliable CTR lift in Backlinko's newer data — the app offers them as variants for info intent but never claims they're magic.
    • AI Overviews cut clicks on informational results by 34.5% (Ahrefs, 300K keywords, Apr 2025; their Dec 2025 update measured −58%). That's why Info-intent pages are tagged "partial recovery only" — their job is authority and citations, not clicks.
    • Position still beats everything. The per-page benchmark math uses the same healthcare-conservative CTR-by-position curve as seo/data/analyze_gsc.py, so the app, the analysis, and the monthly report all agree with each other.

    Sources: Backlinko organic CTR study · Ahrefs, AI Overviews reduce clicks · your own 16-month GSC export (seo/data/2026-07-21/) — the only source that knows this site.

    End of day — ship it

    same order, every batch day
    1. Clear any ⚠ yellow items first — verify the fact or use the safe wording (clinical wording → the page's physician reviewer; scheduling promises → Joe; amenities → ops)
    2. Copy batch for change-log (button in the bottom bar)
    3. Paste into seo/change-log.md
    4. Enter each title & meta in Rank Math — change nothing else on those pages
    5. Add a GSC annotation the same day (e.g. “Batch 1 spine titles — Jul 23”)
    6. Spot-check 3 pages on your actual phone — stale content means the mobile bug: tell Paul

    Then you're done — really done. Don't touch those pages again for 14 days. The waiting is how we measure what worked.
