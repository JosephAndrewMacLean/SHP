# Announcement email — to referring offices

**Send:** Sept 1 · **From:** Kristen or the practice, not marketing
**Status:** DRAFT — requires Dr. Heyl's and Operations' review
**Owner:** Randall (drafting) · **Sender:** Kristen

---

## Design notes

- **Plain and short.** This goes to busy referral coordinators and physicians. It's a notification,
  not a brochure.
- One clear point: **he has availability.**
- Portrait, the referral phone number, and a link to the one-sheet PDF.
- Send from a real person with a real reply-to address. Referral coordinators reply to these with
  practical questions, and someone needs to answer.
