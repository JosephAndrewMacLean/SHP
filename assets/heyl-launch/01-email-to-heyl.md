**Subject:** Five quick things before we build your profile

Dr. Heyl,

Thanks for the onboarding conversation — we have almost everything we need. Five things are still
open, and each one is blocking something specific on our end. Short answers are fine.

**1. What's your exact first day seeing patients?**
We have September 1 as the working target. Every profile, listing, and announcement gets dated off
this, so we'd rather post it once and correctly.

**2. Which locations and days are final, and roughly how many new patients can you take a week?**
Our working understanding: Livonia Monday and Tuesday, Sterling Heights all day Wednesday, with a
possible Friday clinic and Thursday/Friday procedure days. If that's right, just say so. The
patient-count question isn't a target we'll hold you to — it tells us how hard to push before your
schedule is full, and whether Troy is worth adding later.

**3. Why did you go into pain medicine?**
This is the one we genuinely can't work around. Your CV tells us where you trained; it doesn't tell
a patient why you chose this. A few sentences in your own words is plenty — what drew you in, or a
kind of problem you find satisfying to solve. It's usually the most-read part of a physician's page.

**4. How do you want to review drafts, and how quickly can you turn them around?**
Email, a Word document with tracked changes, or a short call — whatever's fastest for you. Knowing
your turnaround lets us schedule backward from September 1 instead of guessing.

**5. What would make the first 90 days feel successful to you?**
Volume, the mix of conditions, referral relationships, procedure mix — whatever actually matters to
you. We'll measure against your answer, not ours. If we fill your schedule with patients you didn't
want, we've done the job badly, and this is how we avoid that.

**One permission question:** you mentioned growing up in Marine City and playing baseball at Siena
Heights. Both are good, human details for your profile and for local press. May we use them? A yes
or no is all we need.

Two things already on the calendar — flag now if either has changed:
- **Photography: Monday, August 10 at Troy.** We'll send the shot list separately.

  answering "what does a pain specialist actually do?" It's the format that performs best for us,
  and it's about ten extra minutes. Happy to skip it if you'd rather not; we won't ask twice.

Everything we write about your training, credentials, and procedures comes back to you for approval
before it goes anywhere public.
