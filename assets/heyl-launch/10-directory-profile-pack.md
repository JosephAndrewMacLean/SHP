Dr. Jonathan Heyl is a pain medicine physician treating spine, joint and nerve pain in Livonia
and Sterling Heights. Board certified in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation and fellowship
trained in pain medicine at the University of Michigan, he focuses on finding the specific

### Medium bio — 100 words *(Healthgrades, Vitals, WebMD)*
```
Dr. Jonathan Heyl is a pain medicine physician at Synergy Health Partners, seeing patients in
Livonia and Sterling Heights, Michigan. He is board certified in Physical Medicine and
Rehabilitation and board eligible in Pain Medicine.

He completed his Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation residency at the University of Michigan,
where he served as Chief Resident, followed by an ACGME-accredited Pain Medicine fellowship in
the University of Michigan Department of Anesthesiology.

Dr. Heyl treats back and neck pain, sciatica, spinal stenosis, joint pain and peripheral nerve
problems using image-guided procedures and electrodiagnostic testing. His approach centers on
identifying the specific source of pain and what it is preventing a patient from doing.
```

### Long bio — 200 words *(Doximity, anywhere with room)*
```
Dr. Jonathan Heyl is a pain medicine physician at Synergy Health Partners in metro Detroit,
practicing in Livonia and Sterling Heights, Michigan.

He completed his residency in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation at the University of Michigan,
where he served as Chief Resident, and an ACGME-accredited Pain Medicine fellowship in the
University of Michigan Department of Anesthesiology. He is board certified in Physical Medicine
and Rehabilitation and board eligible in Pain Medicine.

Dr. Heyl's practice focuses on focal spine, joint and peripheral nerve problems. He treats
persistent back and neck pain, sciatica and radicular pain, spinal stenosis, sacroiliac joint
pain, peripheral nerve problems including mononeuropathies, complex regional pain syndrome, and
focal headache and facial nerve pain.

His procedural practice includes epidural injections, medial branch blocks, radiofrequency
ablation, sacroiliac and intra-articular joint injections, peripheral nerve blocks, occipital and
genicular nerve blocks, peripheral nerve stimulation and spinal cord stimulation. He also performs
EMG and nerve conduction studies.

The combination of rehabilitation and interventional pain training shapes how he practices: he
works to identify the specific structure generating a patient's pain and the functional goal that
matters to them, then treats accordingly.

He is a member of the International Pain and Spine Intervention Society, the North American Spine
Society, and the American Academy of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation.
```

## Step 3 — Site-by-site notes

### Google Business Profile *(highest value — do it first)*

Practitioner listings for **Livonia** and **Sterling Heights**. Do this in the Google Business
Profile interface; the API access is still pending approval and isn't worth waiting for.

- Category: **Pain management physician**. Secondary: **Physiatrist**.
- Hours must match his actual clinic days at that location, not the building's hours.
- Link to his bio URL, not the homepage.
- Add the portrait and 2–3 environmental shots from the Aug 10 session.
- Fill the services list from the procedures above.

*Our Google listings already drive roughly 1,681 local-pack keywords for the practice. This is the
single highest-return item in the pack.*

### Healthgrades / Vitals / WebMD
Claim first if a profile exists. These auto-generate from NPI data and are often wrong. Medium bio,
full conditions and procedures, portrait, both locations.

### Doximity *(worth extra effort)*
This is where his University of Michigan and Ann Arbor network actually is — and his entire referral
strategy runs through those relationships. Long bio, complete training history, publications from
ResearchGate. **When it's done, hand the connection list to Kristen and Jasmine** for outreach; don't
just build it and leave it.

### Zocdoc
Only if he's being added to the Zocdoc booking flow — confirm with Joe first. Zocdoc costs real money
per patient and our own website converts better at no cost, so this is a business decision, not an
SEO one.

### NPI registry
Confirm the taxonomy code is right and the practice address is current. Wrong data here propagates
into every directory that auto-generates profiles — fixing it upstream saves fixing it six times.

### Association directories *(add by Sept 15)*
IPSIS, NASS, and AAPM&R member listings. Legitimate, durable links from medical organizations — a
category where we currently have none at all.
