## Why bother with a press release

Modest expectations, real reasons:

1. **Links.** We have **no editorial links since 2012** and **zero links from Michigan hospital
   systems or medical associations**. Competitors have 400+ referring domains. A new-physician
   announcement is a legitimate, low-effort way to start fixing that.
2. **Name searches.** When referring offices and patients look him up, more than one credible result
   should exist.
3. **It feeds AI answers.** Independent third-party mentions are part of what generative engines use
   to decide who's real.

Not expecting a feature story. Expecting listings, a local pickup or two, and durable links.

## Rules

- **"Board eligible in Pain Medicine."** Never "board certified in Pain Medicine." A press release
  gets syndicated and cached — an error here is permanent and hard to retract.
- **Never** describe him as an anesthesiologist.
- **No superlatives.** No "leading," "top," "premier," "renowned." Beyond being against our own
  standards, editors cut them.
- **No procedure claims** beyond what Operations confirmed. No kyphoplasty, opioid management, stem
  cell therapy or PRP.
- **No patient stories.** None. Not anonymized.
- **Both quotes are drafts.** Nobody is quoted without approving their own words. If Dr. Heyl wants
  to write his own, that's better than ours.
