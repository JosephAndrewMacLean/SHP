## The brief

**Subject:** Dr. Jonathan Heyl — small paid test, September

Team,

We've added a pain medicine physician, Dr. Jonathan Heyl, in Livonia and Sterling Heights. We'd like
a **small, tightly-targeted test** rather than a launch campaign, and there's a compliance list
attached that matters more than usual.

### What we're trying to do

Fill a new physician's schedule with the right patients. That's a different goal from maximizing
volume — he has finite capacity `[X new patients/week, per Operations]` and a specific clinical
scope. Overfilling with poorly-matched patients is a worse outcome than underfilling.

Organic will carry most of this over time. Paid is here to cover the gap in the first 60–90 days
while pages index and rank.

### Geography

**Livonia and Sterling Heights only.** Not metro-wide.

- **Livonia** — Monday, Tuesday
- **Sterling Heights** — Wednesday

Don't advertise for days he isn't there. **No Troy** — he isn't confirmed at Troy.

*(Separately: our own audit flagged that we have no Troy coverage at all, and Troy is our biggest
under-served area. That's worth a conversation with Joe, but it's not this brief.)*

### Suggested starting terms

| Term | Volume | CPC | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| interventional pain management | 5,400 | $5.11 | High intent, matches his positioning |
| pain management doctor near me | 2,400 | $3.21 | Local intent |
| emg test near me | 1,900 | — | Very specific intent, low competition |
| nerve pain doctor | 320 | — | Cheap, precisely on target |
| sciatica doctor near me | — | — | High intent |
| spinal stenosis specialist | — | — | |

**Prefer specific terms over broad ones.** "Pain management" broad will bring in exactly the patients
he's asked us not to schedule — chronic opioid management and widespread pain. That's wasted spend
*and* a poor experience for the patient. Specific procedure and condition terms qualify themselves.

### Negatives — please add these

```
opioid, opioids, pain pills, pain medication prescription, narcotics
suboxone, methadone, addiction, withdrawal
fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue
kyphoplasty, vertebroplasty
stem cell, regenerative, PRP, platelet rich plasma
medical marijuana, cannabis, CBD
disability, workers comp lawyer, personal injury attorney
free, cheap, no insurance
```

The first two lines are the important ones. Those searchers aren't a fit for his practice, and we
made a specific commitment to him about it.

### 🚫 Never advertise

- **Kyphoplasty or vertebral augmentation**
- **Long-term opioid management**
- **Stem cell therapy**
- **PRP** — hold until Operations approves the workflow
- **Spinal cord stimulation as a headline.** He performs it, but it's not his launch identity and
  shouldn't lead any ad.

### 🚫 Never say in ad copy

- **"Board certified in pain medicine"** — he's board *eligible* until his September exam. This is a
  credentialing-accuracy issue, and ad copy is published claims.
- **"Anesthesiologist"** — his fellowship was in an anesthesiology department; his residency was
  PM&R.
- Any outcome promise — "eliminate your pain," "get relief," "pain-free"
- Any superlative — "best," "top," "leading"

### Ad copy we'd suggest

Approved phrasings, use freely:

- "Board certified in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation"
- "Fellowship trained in pain medicine, University of Michigan"
- "Nerve, spine and joint pain"
- "EMG and nerve testing"
- "Evaluation first — we identify the source before recommending treatment"
- "Accepting new patients in Livonia and Sterling Heights"

That fifth one is worth testing as a headline. It's differentiated, it's true, and it pre-qualifies —
people looking for a same-day injection will self-select out, which is what we want.

### Landing pages

Send traffic to **his bio page**: `synergyhealth.org/providers/jonathan-heyl-do/`

A named physician page converts better for this than a service page, and it's the highest-performing
content type we have — our top physician pages earn 200–360 organic clicks a quarter while the pain
service page earns 50.

For EMG-specific terms, use the EMG page once it's live (mid-September).

*(Noted from our own audit: we don't currently have proper paid landing pages with stripped
navigation. Worth revisiting, but not before we've tested whether this even needs scale.)*

### Budget and measurement

Small to start. `[Joe to set — recommend a test budget, not a campaign budget.]`

**Measure booked new patients, not clicks or form fills.** Liine tracks booked appointments and is
now forwarding to GA4. Please confirm you're seeing his bookings attributed correctly before we

We'd rather see 15 well-matched patients than 40 mixed ones.

### Review point

Two weeks in, let's look at: booked patients, whether they match his clinical scope, and his
remaining capacity. Scale, hold, or stop from there.
