## Rule 2 — His training

His Pain Medicine fellowship was in the **University of Michigan Department of Anesthesiology**.

| ✅ Say | ❌ Never say |
|---|---|
| Completed an ACGME-accredited Pain Medicine fellowship in the University of Michigan Department of Anesthesiology | Trained in anesthesiology |
| Fellowship trained in Pain Medicine at the University of Michigan | Anesthesiologist |
| Completed his Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation residency at the University of Michigan, where he served as Chief Resident | Former anesthesiology resident |

A fellowship housed in an anesthesiology department is not an anesthesiology residency. His
residency was in PM&R. Conflating them misstates his training.

## General claims rules (these already apply to everything we publish)

- **No outcome promises.** No "eliminates pain," "cures," "guaranteed relief." He treats and manages;
  he doesn't promise results.
- **No superiority claims** — no "best," "top," "leading," "#1" — unless it's independently verifiable
  and we hold the substantiation.
- **No statistics without a source we can produce on request.** Don't reuse the "92% report
  significant pain reduction" line; its substantiation is already flagged as a problem.
- **No patient stories, photos, or details without written HIPAA authorization.** No exceptions,
  including anonymized-sounding anecdotes.
- **Don't promise speed we can't deliver.** Same-week access is a real differentiator; only state it
  where scheduling can actually honor it.
- **Grade 6–8 reading level** on anything patient-facing.
