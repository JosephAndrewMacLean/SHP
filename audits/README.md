# Cardinal Audits — Source Material & Live Tracker

**The Cardinal (Power Digital) June 2026 audits are the anchor for all measurement and
opportunity work in this workspace.** Everything the team surfaces — low-hanging fruit,
data pulls, briefs for clinical stakeholders — relates back to what Cardinal found, what
they recommended, and whether the website has moved since.

## Files

- **`cardinal-recommendation-tracker.md`** — the working document: every Cardinal
  recommendation, its current verified status on the live site, and the data source that
  measures it. Re-verify monthly (instructions inside).
- **`cardinal-2026-06/`** — extracted text of the three June 2026 audit deliverables
  (converted from the original .docx/.pptx supplied by Joe on 2026-07-22; originals are
  not committed — slide screenshots and charts live only in the originals):
  - `organic-aio-audit.md` — Organic Search + AI Optimization audit, full integrated
    report: 13 technical pillars, 7 AIO/strategy sections, the integrated 90-day
    roadmap (Phases 1–3 with owners), KPI framework, and the YouTube/video appendix.
  - `creative-ux-audit.md` — Creative + UX audit: website experience, paid landing
    pages, messaging & compliance, organic social, video, prioritized roadmap.
  - `paid-media-audit.md` — Paid Media audit: Google Ads performance, Liine conversion
    overhaul (with recommended conversion values), GEO targeting, campaign
    segmentation decision, per-service-line ad group buildouts, PMax test, roadmap.

`brand/current-state.md` is the narrative digest of these audits; the tracker is the
status ledger. When they disagree, verify against the audit text here.
