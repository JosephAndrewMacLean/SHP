### 1.2 Discrepancies to reconcile before publishing

- **Pain Management minimum: SOP table says 10; Katie said 9.** The formula gives
  5 × 3 × 66% = 9.9. The SOP rounded up; Katie's live recitation ("for pain it is
  nine minimum") rounds down. One rounding rule needs to win — the SOP's own text
  ("rounded as operationally appropriate") currently permits both.
- **Hand / Foot & Ankle minimums: SOP table says 4 each; Katie's recitation suggests
  3** ("it's gonna be three; so foot and ankle, it's three" — 2 FT × 3 × 66% = 3.96).
  Same rounding question. Note the SOP scopes minimums to *full-time* physicians —
  Katie confirmed part-time physicians (e.g., Sorenson) are outside the concurrency
  rule.
- **Flat clinic-day counting is already being second-guessed.** Aug 10 (Recording
  117): "a clinic day in Troy is different than a clinic day in Livonia… Ben Mayo's
  Monday in Livonia does not equal Steve's Thursday in Sterling Heights" — the
  minimums may move from flat day-counts to site/day-type-weighted capacity (work
  assigned to Joe with JT/KT). The SOP's "rounded as operationally appropriate"
  line is where this would land. A separate proposal floated raising ortho+spine
  days from 12 toward 16 (Recording 120) — not decided.
- **Review cadence of the 66% formula** was left open in the Aug 11 meeting
  ("reviewed every five months… or every quarter?"). The SOP only says recalculate
  when headcount changes. Headcount *is* changing: **Dr. Heil starts Sept 1** (pain
  service line), and Acker and Munk are being removed from the clinic-capacity
  report — the table needs a recalculation pass and a stated review interval.
- **Unverified summary claim:** the Aug 11 Orthopedic Scheduling meeting summary
  says "Jeff's and David's clinics will no longer count towards clinic days." Not
  confirmed in the transcript excerpts pulled so far — verify with Katie before
  treating it as policy (it materially changes the ortho denominator).

### 2.2 Tension to resolve: template capacity vs. SOP thresholds

The SOP flags >5 patients/hour (§3A) and >2 new patients/hour (§3B) for review.
The template rebuild described Aug 11 for Jeff/Steve/David reportedly builds **four
new-patient slots and four follow-up slots per hour block** when PA support exists
(Disability Workflow meeting action item; Kelly's "four now, over eight" comment).
Taken literally that's 8 slots/hour and 4 NP/hour — double both SOP thresholds.
Either "hour block" actually means a half-day session, or the thresholds are now
PA-conditional and §3A/§3B need re-writing to match the templates. **Verify with
Kelly before editing either document** — this is the biggest internal contradiction
found this week.
