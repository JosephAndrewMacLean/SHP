## Purpose

To establish a standardized, proactive process for daily clinic schedule utilization
review and optimization for physicians and PAs in order to maximize access, improve
provider efficiency, balance workloads, and achieve targeted utilization benchmarks.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all ambulatory clinic schedules for physicians and physician
assistants (PAs). It covers daily review of same-day and near-term schedules,
utilization assessment, schedule balancing, provider communication, staffing
coordination, provider coverage, and escalation pathways.

## Roles & Responsibilities

**Clinic Scheduling Manager / Director**

- Owns daily clinic utilization review process
- Runs utilization reports and identifies gaps or risks
- Coordinates schedule optimization actions
- Communicates changes to physicians, office managers, PA leadership, and marketing as needed
- Coordinates PA support assignments for fully utilized physician schedules

**Clinic Managers / Office Managers**

- Adjust clinic staffing based on schedule changes
- Ensure front desk and MA staffing align with revised schedules
- Communicate operational changes to clinic teams

**PA Manager**

- Assesses and deploys PA support when requested
- Ensures PA schedules comply with utilization standards
- Coordinates PA coverage for physician time away (PTA)
- Adjusts PA clinic schedules based on utilization requirements

**Marketing Team**

- Provides support for low-utilization schedules through new patient campaigns,
  provider-level promotion, and PL events when requested

**Physicians & PAs**

- Are informed of schedule adjustments that impact arrival/departure times or clinic flow
- Collaborate with leadership to maintain balanced and effective schedules

## Procedure

### 1. Daily Schedule Review Timing

On a daily basis, the Clinic Scheduling Manager/Director will review:

- Same-day schedules
- Next two (2) business day schedules

The review applies to all physicians and PAs scheduled in clinic.

### 2. Overall Utilization Assessment

- Review total scheduled patients versus total available appointment slots
- Calculate total utilization percentage
- Target utilization goal: **≥ 90%**

Utilization data should be reviewed using the standardized Clinic Utilization Report
by Service Line.

### 3. Physician Schedule Assessment

Evaluate the following elements closely:

#### A. Patients Per Hour

- Identify if the physician is scheduled over five (5) patients per hour.
- Assess duration:
  - If sustained for more than two (2) consecutive hours, determine whether:
    - PA support is in place
    - The visit mix is appropriate for the workload

#### B. New Patients Per Hour

- Identify if there are more than two (2) new patients scheduled per hour.
- Assess whether:
  - Appropriate clinical support is assigned
  - New patients are being scheduled into reserved new patient appointment slots

#### C. Physician Double Booking Standard

**Orthopedic and Spine Physicians**

- Maximum double-booking allowance is two (2) appointments per hour unless otherwise
  approved by the physician.
- Double-booking is permitted for follow-up appointments only.
- New patient appointment slots must remain reserved exclusively for new patients.
- New patient appointments may not be double-booked except with physician approval.

Schedule adjustments should be made as necessary to maintain provider efficiency and
preserve access for new patients.

### 4. PA Schedule Standards

- PAs may have no more than two (2) new patients per hour.
- No double-booking of new patients except for acute injury visits.
- Maximum double-booking rate of 15% or five (5) double-booked visits per ten-hour shift.

Schedules exceeding these thresholds should be reviewed and rebalanced.

### 5. Physician Time Away (PTA) Coverage

For full-time Orthopedic and Spine physicians:

- If a physician's clinic schedule is at least 70% filled at the time of approved PTA,
  PA coverage may be assigned.
- The PA team will make every reasonable effort to mirror the physician's normal clinic
  days, hours, and location.
- Coverage schedules should be structured to minimize disruption and reduce confusion
  for patients, providers, and clinic staff.
- PA coverage assignments will be coordinated by the PA Manager in collaboration with
  Clinic Scheduling Leadership.

### 6. PA Support Escalation & High Utilization Management

Clinic utilization must be reviewed daily.

**When a physician reaches 100% clinic utilization:**

- The Clinic Scheduling Director and PA Manager will review available PA resources.
- PAs may be reassigned to support the physician's clinic.
- Follow-up appointments may be redirected into available PA clinic openings when
  clinically appropriate.
- Support assignments should prioritize maintaining patient access and reducing
  scheduling delays.

**When a PA clinic falls to 85% utilization or lower:**

- The Director of Clinical Scheduling may request schedule adjustments.
- The PA Manager will evaluate and modify PA schedules as appropriate.
- Adjustments may include clinic consolidation, schedule realignment, or reassignment
  of appointment capacity.

**For any physician with less than 70% schedule utilization:**

*Schedule Condensing*

- Identify opportunities to condense schedules.
- More than four (4) open appointment slots in a row should trigger condensing efforts.
- Move patients earlier or later in the day where appropriate.

*Marketing & Growth Support*

- Communicate low-utilization findings to the Marketing Team.
- Request targeted support such as:
  - New patient campaigns
  - Provider-level promotion
  - Provider Liaison (PL) events

## Communication & Staffing Adjustments

If schedule optimization results in a physician:

- Arriving earlier, or
- Leaving earlier than originally scheduled

The following steps are required:

- Direct communication with the physician regarding the change
- Notification to the Office Manager
- Adjustment of clinic staffing levels as needed
- Clear communication to all impacted clinic staff

## Monitoring & Accountability

- Clinic utilization metrics will be reviewed regularly by Clinical Operations Leadership.
- Trends in utilization, double-booking, provider access, and PA support utilization
  will be monitored.
- Repeated variances may result in provider-level or clinic-level optimization plans.
